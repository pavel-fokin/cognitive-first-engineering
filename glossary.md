### Abstraction
A compression mechanism that reduces multiple cognitive objects into one. A good abstraction *earns* its slot by reliably hiding complexity. A bad abstraction leaks, forcing the holder to track both the abstraction and its internals — costing more than it saves.

### Cognitive Debt
The accumulating cost of systems that exceed human comprehension limits. Unlike technical debt (which slows execution), cognitive debt degrades the ability to reason about the system at all. It compounds silently until a failure reveals it.

### Cognitive Object
A unit of meaning that occupies one slot in working memory. May be a single symbol or a compressed structure (chunk). The cost of a system is measured in cognitive objects required to hold it in mind, not lines of code or number of components.
